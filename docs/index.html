Gamexercise

Walk to reign. A pixel-art RPG powered by your real-world steps.

About

You were once the realm's greatest hero. A curse stole your strength. Now your daily steps — read straight from Apple HealthKit — are the only thing that can restore it.

Every step you take feeds your character. Walk to level up, earn coins from real movement, dress your hero in outfits you've earned, and watch your form transform when your race awakens.

Privacy

Gamexercise collects no personal data. Your HealthKit step counts never leave your device. There are no user accounts, no ads, no analytics.

Anonymous crash diagnostics are sent through Sentry to help us fix bugs — these never include personally identifying information and can be disabled in iOS Settings.

  • HealthKit step data (built in to every modern iPhone)
